SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION
17 CFR Part 240
[Release No. 34-BASELINE; File No. S7-BASELINE]
RIN 3235-BASELINE

RETENTION OF RECORDS BY BROKERS AND DEALERS
(BASELINE VERSION - Pre-Amendment)

AGENCY: Securities and Exchange Commission.
ACTION: Final Rule (Baseline).

SUMMARY: The Securities and Exchange Commission is publishing this baseline version of Rule 17a-4 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 for reference purposes. This document reflects the rule requirements in effect prior to the 2024-2025 amendment cycle.

EFFECTIVE DATE: This baseline version reflects requirements effective as of January 1, 2023.

SECTION 1. GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

1.1 Applicability
Every member, broker, and dealer subject to this section shall preserve for a period of not less than three years, the first two years in an accessible place, the following records:

(a) All records required to be made pursuant to paragraphs (a)(1) through (a)(18) of Rule 17a-3.

(b) All trial balances, computations of aggregate indebtedness and net capital, all supporting schedules prepared in connection with such computations, work papers, and all other records required to demonstrate the accuracy of such computations.

(c) All check books, bank statements, cancelled checks, and cash reconciliations.

(d) All bills receivable or payable, confirmations, and all other records required to be made pursuant to Rule 17a-3 which are not described in paragraphs (b)(1) through (b)(6) of this section.

1.2 Six-Year Retention Requirements
Every member, broker, and dealer subject to this section shall preserve for a period of not less than six years, the first two years in an accessible place:

(a) All partnership articles, articles of incorporation, minute books, and stock certificate books of the member, broker, or dealer.

(b) All Forms BD and amendments thereto filed with the Commission.

(c) All written agreements or other documents which are subject to Rule 17a-3(a)(10).

(d) All records required pursuant to Rule 17a-3(a)(15) and (16) relating to customer complaints.

SECTION 2. ELECTRONIC STORAGE REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Non-Rewriteable, Non-Erasable Format
Any electronic storage media utilized shall meet the following requirements:

(a) The electronic storage media must preserve the records exclusively in a non-rewriteable, non-erasable format. This requirement is commonly referred to as a WORM (Write Once Read Many) requirement.

(b) The member, broker, or dealer shall verify automatically the quality and accuracy of the electronic storage media recording process.

(c) The member, broker, or dealer shall serialize the original and, if applicable, duplicate units of storage media, and shall time-date for the required period of retention the information placed on such electronic storage media.

2.2 Duplicate Copy Requirement
Members, brokers, and dealers that employ electronic storage media shall:

(a) Maintain, keep current, and provide promptly to the Commission or any self-regulatory organization of which it is a member, upon request, a listing of the organization responsible for maintaining and enforcing a designated third-party escrow arrangement.

(b) Store a duplicate copy of the electronic records in a separate facility. The duplicate copy may be stored on or off the premises of the primary storage location.

(c) Ensure that the electronic records are indexed and that the index is available to the Commission upon request.

2.3 Third-Party Escrow Arrangements
Members, brokers, and dealers that employ electronic storage media may, in lieu of storing a duplicate copy at a separate facility, enter into an escrow arrangement with a designated third party who has access to and the ability to download information from the member's, broker's, or dealer's electronic storage media to any acceptable medium. Such escrow arrangement must provide that:

(a) A copy of the escrow agreement be provided to the Commission or the designated examining authority upon request.

(b) The third party provides the Commission or the designated examining authority with access to such records upon request.

SECTION 3. ACCESSIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

3.1 Prompt Access
Every member, broker, and dealer subject to this section shall maintain records required to be maintained pursuant to this section so that such records are at all times available for immediate inspection and copying by the Commission, any state securities regulatory authority, and any self-regulatory organization of which it is a member.

3.2 Index Requirement
The member, broker, or dealer shall maintain and preserve, at minimum, a listing by type of media of the electronic storage media, together with a notation of the location of the storage media, the date stored, the time period covered by the records, and the location where the index to such records is stored.

3.3 Output Capability
The member, broker, or dealer shall have the capacity to produce immediately upon request by the Commission an output of the entire filing system to an external computer system or medium, such as magnetic tape, in an industry-standard format.

SECTION 4. COMMUNICATIONS RECORDS

4.1 Business Communications
Business-related communications made through electronic communications media, including but not limited to email and instant messages transmitted or received by the member, broker, or dealer, and related to its business as such, shall be preserved for a period of three years.

4.2 Preservation Format
Such records shall be preserved in a form that reflects the original message including header information, sender, recipient, date, and time stamps.

4.3 Review and Supervision
Records of business communications must be subject to review by appropriate supervisory personnel and must be preserved in a manner that is consistent with the requirements of this section.

SECTION 5. OBLIGATIONS UPON TERMINATION OF BUSINESS

5.1 Transfer of Records
In the event a member, broker, or dealer ceases to conduct business as such, such member, broker, or dealer shall arrange for the preservation of the records required to be maintained and preserved pursuant to this section for the remainder of the required retention period.

5.2 Notification
The member, broker, or dealer shall inform the Commission of the arrangements it has made for the preservation of records at least 30 days prior to ceasing business operations.

5.3 Successor Obligations
If any other member, broker, or dealer or other organization takes over the business of the former member, broker, or dealer, such successor shall preserve and maintain such records for the periods required under this section.

SECTION 6. EXAMINATION AUTHORITY

6.1 Commission Access
Every member, broker, and dealer subject to this section shall, upon request of any representative of the Commission or of a registered securities association, make available promptly for examination or copying:

(a) All records required to be maintained pursuant to this section.

(b) All other books and records of the member, broker, or dealer.

6.2 Response Timeline
Members, brokers, and dealers must produce requested records within a reasonable time. For electronically stored records, the standard response time for Commission requests is no more than five business days unless a longer period is specifically authorized.
